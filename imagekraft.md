# Privacy Policy for ImageKraft

**Last Updated: October 1, 2026**

ImageKraft was previously named Lazy's Image Tools. This policy covers version 2.0 and later. Versions 1.x are covered by the [Lazy's Image Tools policy](lazys-image-tools.md).

## Overview
ImageKraft is an image toolkit for macOS. Your images are processed on your Mac and are never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no analytics, no tracking and no account.

## Data Collection
**The app collects no personal information.**

We do not collect, and have no access to:
- Your images or anything in them
- Personal information such as your name, email address or Apple ID
- Usage data or analytics
- Crash reports
- Location data
- Your settings

## How The App Works
- All image processing happens on your Mac, including AI background removal
- Your images are never uploaded to any server
- No user accounts or registration
- No tracking, analytics or advertising tools are included

## Your Images
- Images you work on stay on your Mac
- The app only reads images you give it: by choosing them in a file dialog, dragging them in, pasting them, opening them from Finder, or sending them through agent access if you have turned that on
- Results are saved only where you choose
- Temporary working copies are kept in the app's own private folder and are removed by the app or by macOS

## When The App Uses The Network
The app makes exactly one kind of internet connection: the optional HD model download. There are no background connections, update checks or telemetry.

### Downloading the HD background removal model (optional)
Background Remover works out of the box with a model built into macOS. You can choose to download a more accurate HD model (about 490 MB). The download starts only when you click Download.

To confirm the app was installed from the Mac App Store, the app sends Apple's signed proof of purchase for the app (the StoreKit "AppTransaction") to our download server. The proof contains the app's identifier and version, the original purchase date and a device verification value generated by Apple. It does not contain your name, email address or Apple ID.

The server checks Apple's signature and sends back the model file. It stores nothing: there is no database, no saved proof, no account and no identifier. We do not store or log your proof of purchase or any information that identifies you.

macOS may ask you to sign in to the App Store before the download. That sign-in is handled by Apple, not by the app.

The download server runs on Cloudflare. Cloudflare, as the host, processes the connection, including your IP address, to deliver the file, under [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Once downloaded, the model runs entirely on your Mac. No image is sent anywhere.

## Agent Access (optional, off by default)
In Preferences > Agents you can let AI coding agents on your Mac (such as Claude Code or Codex) use ImageKraft's tools.

- When it is on, the app accepts connections only from your own Mac (the local address 127.0.0.1). It is not reachable from your network or the internet.
- Requests must carry a private access token. The app writes the token and a skill named `imagekraft` into a folder named `imagekraft` inside the folder you pick (for example `~/.claude/skills/imagekraft`). You can reset the token or turn access off at any time.
- Images an agent sends are processed on your Mac and returned to that agent. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
- While access is on, the app keeps running in the menu bar after its window is closed.

What an AI agent does with your images before or after it uses ImageKraft is governed by that agent's own privacy policy, not this one.

## Links That Open Other Apps
In the Metadata tool, "Open in Maps" hands a photo's GPS coordinates to the Maps app. This happens only when you click it. The app itself does not connect to the internet for this.

## Third-Party Services
- **Cloudflare**: hosts the optional HD model download described above. It is used for nothing else.
- **Apple**: the app is distributed through the Mac App Store.

The app contains no:
- Analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
- Crash reporting tools (Crashlytics, Sentry, etc.)
- Advertising networks
- Social media integrations

## App Sandbox
The app runs in Apple's App Sandbox. It can only reach:
- Files and folders you explicitly choose or give to it
- The network, used only as described above

## Data Stored On Your Mac
The app stores the following, only on your Mac:
- Your preferences (appearance, last used settings)
- The HD model, if you downloaded it
- The agent access token and the list of folders where you installed the skill, if you use agent access

These live in the app's sandbox container. To remove them, delete the app and then delete the folder `~/Library/Containers/com.lazydevgames.imagetools`. To remove an installed skill, delete its `imagekraft` folder from the agent's skills folder.

## Children's Privacy
The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Your Rights
We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete.
