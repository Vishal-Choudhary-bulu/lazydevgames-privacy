# Privacy Policy for PicReady

**Effective date: October 14, 2026**

PicReady was previously named ImageKraft Lite.

This Privacy Policy describes how LazyDev Games ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the PicReady mobile application for iOS and Android (the "App").

## Information We Collect

LazyDev Games does not receive your photos and does not collect personal information through the App. The App has no account, and we do not run our own analytics or crash reporting.

The App does include Google's advertising SDK, which collects some information from your device to show ads. This is described under [Advertising](#advertising) below.

## Your Photos

All photo processing takes place on your device. Photos you open in the App are not uploaded to us or to any third party, and are not used for advertising. The App accesses only the photos you select or share to it.

## Advertising

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and personalize ads (where you allow it) and to prevent fraud, Google's advertising SDK may collect:

- Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising identifier and app set ID
- Your IP address, from which an approximate location may be inferred
- Device and operating system details, such as model, language and OS version
- Interactions with ads, such as views and taps
- Diagnostic and performance information about the advertising SDK

This information is collected and used by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and Google may share it with its advertising partners as described there. Learn more about [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you, and we do not sell personal information.

## Your Choices

Where required by law, the App asks for your consent to personalized advertising when you first open it, and Settings > Privacy choices appears in the App so you can change your choice at any time. On iOS, the App also asks whether it may track you, and you can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path varies by device).

Changing these choices affects how ads are shown from then on. It does not erase information Google has already collected; see [Data Retention and Deletion](#data-retention-and-deletion).

## Data Stored on Your Device

App preferences, such as saved settings and presets, are stored only on your device. Photos the App is working on are kept in the App's temporary storage on your device and are not sent anywhere.

## Data Retention and Deletion

- **On your device:** you can remove the App's data at any time by clearing its storage (on Android, Settings > Apps > PicReady > Storage > Clear storage) or by deleting the App. Photos you save or share from the App stay in your gallery or the app you shared them to until you delete them there.
- **With us:** we do not hold any of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete.
- **Advertising data:** Google decides how long it keeps the information its advertising SDK collects, as described in [How Google retains data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention). You can review and delete data linked to your Google Account at [My Google Activity](https://myactivity.google.com), manage ad personalization at [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com), and make other privacy requests through the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy#infochoices).

## Security

Because photo processing happens on your device and the App has no server of its own, your photos are never transmitted by us. Information collected by Google's advertising SDK is sent to Google over encrypted connections.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be revised.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at help@lazydevtools.com.
