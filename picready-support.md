# PicReady Support

PicReady compresses, resizes, crops and converts photos on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded.

## Contact

Email **help@lazydevtools.com**.

## Common questions

**Why is my photo slightly under the size I picked?**
PicReady never goes over your limit, so it may land a little under it.

**Where are my photos saved?**
Saved photos go to your photo library (iOS) or gallery (Android). Your original photo is never changed.

**Does PicReady need the internet?**
No. Editing works fully offline. An internet connection is only used to show ads.

**How do I change my ad privacy choices?**
Open Settings > Privacy choices in the app (shown where required by law). On iOS you can also change tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
