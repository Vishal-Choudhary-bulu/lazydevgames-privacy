# Privacy Policy for ImageKraft Lite

**Last Updated: October 11, 2026**

## Overview
ImageKraft Lite is a photo app for iPhone and Android that makes a photo fit a requirement: a file size, pixel dimensions, a format, or no location data. Your photos are edited on your phone and are never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account and no analytics.

The app is free and shows ads, provided by Google AdMob. Ads are the only part of the app that uses the internet. This policy explains what that means.

## Data Collection
**We collect no personal information.**

We do not collect, and have no access to:
- Your photos or anything in them
- Personal information such as your name, email address or phone number
- Usage data or analytics
- Crash reports
- Your location
- Your saved settings

The ads in the app are provided by Google, which collects some data to show them. See "Advertising" below.

## Your Photos
- All editing happens on your phone, including shrinking, resizing, cropping, format changes and removing location data
- Your photos are never uploaded to any server, and are never shown to or used by the ad provider
- The app only opens photos you pick yourself in the system photo picker, or share to it from another app. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library
- The original photo is never changed. The app makes a new copy
- A new copy is saved to your photo library only when you tap Save photo (on Android, in Pictures/ImageKraft). On iPhone, the app asks for permission to add photos the first time you save
- A copy leaves your phone only when you choose Share and pick where to send it
- Working copies are kept in the app's own private folder and are removed by the app or by the system

## Advertising
The app shows one ad, on the screen you see while a photo is being processed. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app may collect:
- Your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking)
- Your IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out
- Device and app information, such as the device model, operating system version and app version
- How you interact with ads, such as whether you tap one

This data goes to Google, not to us. Your photos, file names and saved settings are never part of it.

**Your choices:**
- **Consent:** where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form when it first opens. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices
- **iPhone tracking:** the app asks for permission to track with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads can still appear but are not personalized using your advertising identifier
- **Android:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Settings under Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads)

How Google uses this data is described in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Third-Party Services
- **Google AdMob** and **Google User Messaging Platform**: show ads and ask for ad consent, as described above
- **Apple App Store** and **Google Play**: distribute the app

The app contains no:
- Analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
- Crash reporting tools (Crashlytics, Sentry, etc.)
- Social media integrations
- Accounts or sign-in

## Data Stored On Your Phone
The app stores the following, only on your phone:
- Your saved settings (presets you create)
- Your language choice
- Your ad consent choice (stored by Google's consent software)

To remove them, delete the app.

## Children's Privacy
ImageKraft Lite is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your Rights
We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. For data collected by Google for ads, use the choices above or [Google's privacy controls](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

## Changes to This Policy
If our privacy practices change, we will update this policy and the "Last Updated" date.
