# Privacy Policy for ImageKraft Lite

**Effective date: October 11, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how LazyDev Games ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the ImageKraft Lite mobile application for iOS and Android (the "App").

## Information We Collect

We do not collect, store or have access to any personal information. The App does not require an account and contains no analytics or crash reporting.

## Your Photos

All photo processing takes place on your device. Photos you open in the App are not uploaded to us or to any third party, and are not used for advertising. The App accesses only the photos you select or share to it.

## Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and operating system details, and interactions with ads. This information is collected and processed by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Learn more about [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Your Choices

Where required by law, the App requests your consent for personalized advertising when first opened. You can change this choice at any time in the App under Settings > Privacy choices. You can also limit ad tracking in your device settings: on iOS through Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and on Android through Settings > Privacy > Ads.

## Data Stored on Your Device

App preferences, such as saved settings, are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the App.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be revised.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at help@lazydevtools.com.
